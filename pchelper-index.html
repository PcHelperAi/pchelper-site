PC Helper

A voice assistant for your own PC, with a companion Android app that keeps your phone and PC working together.

PC Helper runs on Windows and listens for a wake word ("Jarvis") or a typed command, then does things on your computer for you: opening apps, searching, answering questions, controlling music, taking notes, setting reminders and timers, and more. It uses an AI model for understanding open-ended requests, and a small set of fast built-in commands for the things you ask most.

Phone companion app

The Android app pairs with the PC over an end-to-end encrypted link, so you can ask your PC to do things from your phone ("on my pc, open spotify"), and ask your phone to do things from the PC (read notifications, answer calls, find your phone). Everything is encrypted between the two devices; nothing routes through a third party that can read it.

Cross-device accounts

An optional account lets your custom commands, learned words and AI/voice keys follow you to every PC and phone you log into, encrypted with a key derived from your own password — the account's own backend never sees the plaintext keys, only the encrypted blobs.
